MAT 186: Introduction to Data Science

Interactive Lecture Notes

Welcome to the official OER Lecture Notes for MAT 186. These materials are designed to provide a theoretical and practical foundation in Data Science using R and Quarto.

Official Course Description

This course introduces the basic ideas and techniques of data science including: exploratory data analysis; experimental design and sampling; relationships between one and several variables including single and multiple regression and two-way tables; sampling distributions; inferential statistics for means, proportions, and regression coefficients; and simple ANOVA.

The course includes a significant computer component using the software package R.

Prerequisite: Placement level 4 (see DCC Math Placement Table) or MAT 184 with a grade of C or better.

Key Aspects of MAT 186 and the Program

Career Readiness & Microcredentials

MAT 186 is a core component of our three-course Data Analysis microcredential. This program is specifically designed to prepare students for careers as junior data analysts, focusing on practical applications for business and marketing analytics.

  • Course Focus: We cover data visualization, data cleaning, and statistical analysis to interpret complex data patterns.
  • Technical Skills: Students master industry-standard tools and programming languages, specifically R, RStudio, and Quarto.

Institutional Distinction

Dutchess Community College (DCC) is proud to be the first community college in the nation with an American Statistical Association (ASA) Student Chapter. This connection provides our students with unique networking and professional development opportunities in the field of statistics.

Course Goals

  • Master the Data Science workflow (Import, Tidy, Transform, Visualize, Model, Communicate).
  • Build reproducible reports using Quarto and GitHub.
  • Apply statistical inference to real-world datasets.
